About

Founder

Ahmed is a California-licensed Civil Engineer with experience in transportation and roadway design, public infrastructure, private development and grading, construction, pavement rehabilitation, and pavement materials.

He holds a Ph.D. in Civil Engineering from Temple University, where his research focused on pavement materials, rehabilitation, and sustainable applications of recycled materials.
